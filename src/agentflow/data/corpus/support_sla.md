# Support SLA

## Response times
Priority 1 tickets get a first response within 4 hours. Priority 2 tickets within 8 hours. Standard tickets within 24 hours.

## Hours
Support is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. Priority 1 has 24 hour cover.

## Escalation
Tickets with no response after the SLA window are escalated to the support lead automatically.
