# Product notes

## Atlas
Atlas is the entry-level tracker. It has a warranty of 2 years and a battery life of 10 days.

## Beacon
Beacon is the mid-range tracker with GPS. It has a warranty of 3 years and a battery life of 6 days.

## Cadence
Cadence is the fitness band. It has a warranty of 1 year and is water resistant to 50 metres.

## Delta
Delta is the premium model with a sapphire display. It has a warranty of 5 years and a battery life of 14 days.
