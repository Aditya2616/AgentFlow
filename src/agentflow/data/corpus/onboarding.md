# Onboarding guide

## First day
New hires receive a laptop on day 1 and are assigned an onboarding buddy for the first month.

## Security training
Security training must be completed within 7 days of the start date. Access to production systems is granted only after it is done.

## 30-60-90 plan
By day 30 the new hire ships a small fix, by day 60 owns a feature, by day 90 presents a retrospective to the team.
