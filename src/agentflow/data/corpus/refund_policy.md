# Refund policy

## Refund window
Customers have 30 days from the delivery date to request a refund on physical goods. Digital goods can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. Processing takes 5 business days after the returned item is received.

## Exceptions
Items marked final sale cannot be refunded. Damaged items are refunded in full, including shipping, if reported within 7 days.
